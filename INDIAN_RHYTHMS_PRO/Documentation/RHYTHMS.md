Teen Taal main bol scaffold: Dha Dhin Dhin Dha / Dha Dhin Dhin Dha / Na Tin Tin Ta / Ta Dhin Dhin Dha. Sam at 1, tali at 5 and 13, khali at 9; the synthetic Na substitutes the lighter first bol of the khali vibhag. Rupak: Tin Tin Na / Dhin Na / Dhin Na; khali and sam coincide at beat 1; tali at 4 and 6. Keherwa: Dha Ge Na Tin / Na Ke Dhin Na. Dadra: Dha Dhin Na / Dha Tin Na. Many valid traditional variants exist; these are selected scaffolds with original production variations, not a scholarly reference or a claim of authentic acoustic performance.

MIDI includes Intro/Main/Variation A/Variation B/Fill/Break/Ending sections at 1, 2, 4 and 8 complete cycles. Sparse intros and breaks preserve cycle duration; fills add subdivisions, variations change strokes/dynamics. Teen Taal and Rupak Main retain their listed scaffolds; other sections intentionally depart from them. Most melodic dance arrangements use modest offbeat swing; it is disabled for Teen Taal and Rupak. Random timing is deterministic and bounded; cycle boundaries remain exact. Sound-layer dynamics and note velocity are separate from metric accents.

Loop duration = bars × numerator × 4/denominator × stored MIDI microseconds-per-quarter / 1,000,000. WAV frame counts round that duration once to the nearest 48 kHz sample. Integer MIDI tempos differ minutely from ideal rational BPM; WAV and MIDI share the same stored tempo.
